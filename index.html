CapiTracker

To Keep the Budget Lekker

A Sheet-backed budget tracker for South African bank statements. Built around pay-cycle windows (25th → 25th by default), not calendar months — so what you see actually matches how you spend.

What it does

  • · Auto-ingests bank CSV attachments from your Gmail
  • · Classifies transactions with simple rules you control
  • · Compares budget vs actuals over your pay cycle, not the calendar month
  • · Merges main account + credit card into one ledger
  • · Privacy mode — blur amounts with one click for screen-sharing
  • · No build step. No npm. Single HTML file + a Google Sheet.
